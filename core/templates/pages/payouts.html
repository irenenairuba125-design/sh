Get paid to your mobile money.

How you earn

  1. A student subscribes to your course and their payment is confirmed.
  2. The platform keeps a {{ site_settings.platform_fee_percent }}% fee. The rest is added to your balance, shown on your teacher dashboard.
  3. We send your balance to the mobile-money number on your account, and record the payout with its transaction reference so you can see it.

Good to know

  • Add a mobile-money number on your account page. Payouts go to that number.
  • We aim to pay out weekly. Every payout appears in the Payouts table on your dashboard with the date and reference.
  • Only verified instructors can publish courses and earn. Verification is done by an admin after you sign up.
