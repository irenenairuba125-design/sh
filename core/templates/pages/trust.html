Trust & safety

How we keep learning and payments safe.

Verified instructors

Anyone can apply to teach, but an admin verifies each instructor before their courses go live. Verified instructors carry a badge on their course pages.

Payments

Online payments are handled by Pesapal. Manual mobile-money payments are checked by an admin against the transaction ID before a course is unlocked, and a transaction ID can only be used once. We never see your mobile-money PIN.

Protected lessons

Lesson videos and notes are only served to people who have paid for the course. There is no public link to the files, so they cannot be shared around.

Cancelling

There is no automatic renewal. Your access lasts for the period you paid for and then ends unless you choose to pay again.

Report a problem

If you see content that is wrong, unsafe or unfair, or you have a payment problem, contact us{% if site_settings.support_email %} at {{ site_settings.support_email }}{% endif %}{% if site_settings.support_phone %} or on {{ site_settings.support_phone }}{% endif %}, and include the course name and what happened.
