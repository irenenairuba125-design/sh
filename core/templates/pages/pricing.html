Pricing

Simple, and paid with mobile money.

If you want to learn

Joining is free. You only pay when you subscribe to a course, and every course has a free preview lesson so you can try before you pay. Each teacher sets the price of their own course, shown on the course page in Uganda shillings. Courses billed monthly show /month.

  • Pay with MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Visa or Mastercard.
  • There is no automatic renewal: your access lasts for the period you paid for, then it simply ends unless you pay again.
  • Download lessons and notes for offline study.

If you want to teach

It is free to create an account and build courses. You set your own price. When a student subscribes, the platform keeps a {{ site_settings.platform_fee_percent }}% fee and the rest is yours. For example, on a UGX 15,000 subscription you earn UGX {{ example_net }}.
