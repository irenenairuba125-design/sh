Privacy

A short, plain-language starting point. Have it reviewed before you launch.

What we collect

Your username, email address and phone number; for instructors also the location, short bio and mobile-money number you provide; and a record of your payments, lessons and quiz results.

Why

To run your account, unlock the courses you paid for, confirm payments, pay instructors and issue certificates.

Who sees it

Instructors see the usernames of their paying students and their quiz results. Payment providers process your payment. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices
