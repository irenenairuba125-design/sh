About {{ site_settings.site_name }}

A marketplace for practical law skills, paid for by mobile money.

Law school teaches the theory. The skills that get you through moot court, the Bar Course, a clerkship and your first year in practice, such as drafting a plaint, briefing a case or arguing a submission, are often learned on the job. {{ site_settings.site_name }} puts those skills into short lessons taught by people who have done the work.

Why mobile money

Most students already pay with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, and many study on a phone with limited data. So subscriptions are monthly, lessons can be downloaded for offline study, and the data size of a lesson is shown before you download it.

Two doors, one house

Students learn. Practising advocates and lecturers teach and get paid. Everyone is in the same place.
